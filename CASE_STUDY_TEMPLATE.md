# Marketplace Case Study Template

## Situation
Describe the marketplace, catalog size, and business problem without exposing confidential client information.

## Problem
What prevented the listing, catalog, variation, or upload from working correctly?

## Audit
Document the checks performed across product data, marketplace attributes, identifiers, variations, content, and upload method.

## Solution
Explain the corrected workflow and source-data changes.

## Verification
Record how the live listing/catalog was checked after processing.

## Outcome
Use only verified outcomes. Do not invent revenue, ranking, or percentage improvements.

## Privacy
Remove client names, credentials, account IDs, private screenshots, order data, and proprietary exports unless publication was explicitly approved.
